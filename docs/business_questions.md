# Business Questions

The Enterprise AI Business Intelligence Copilot should be able to answer the following business questions.

## Sales Questions

### 1. Total Sales

What is the total sales amount?

### 2. Monthly Sales

What are the sales for each month?

### 3. Quarterly Sales

What are the sales for each quarter?

### 4. Yearly Sales

What are the yearly sales?

### 5. Sales by State

Which states generated the highest sales?

### 6. Sales by Region

Which regions generated the highest sales?

## Product Questions

### 7. Top Products

What are the top-selling products?

### 8. Most Profitable Products

Which products generate the highest profit?

## Customer Questions

### 9. Top Customers

Who are the top customers by revenue?

### 10. Customer Count

How many customers do we have?

## Profit Questions

### 11. Total Profit

What is the total profit?

### 12. Profit by Region

Which region generates the highest profit?

## Comparison Questions

### 13. Kerala vs Karnataka

Compare sales between Kerala and Karnataka.

### 14. Current Month vs Previous Month

How did this month's sales compare with last month's sales?

### 15. Current Year vs Previous Year

How did this year's revenue compare with last year's revenue?
