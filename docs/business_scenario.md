# NovaMart Business Scenario

## 1. Company Name

NovaMart

## 2. Company Type

NovaMart is a fictional retail company operating across multiple states in India.

The company sells consumer products through multiple stores and sales channels.

## 3. Business Objective

NovaMart wants to use an AI-powered Business Intelligence system to understand its business performance.

The company wants to analyze:

- Sales
- Revenue
- Profit
- Customers
- Products
- Regions
- Employees
- Expenses
- Sales targets

## 4. Business Problems

NovaMart currently has large amounts of business data.

Business managers need answers to questions such as:

- How much did we sell this month?
- Which state generated the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Who are our top customers?
- Which region is performing poorly?
- How is this month's sales compared with last month?
- What are the sales trends?
- What could future sales look like?

Finding these answers manually using SQL and spreadsheets takes time.

## 5. Proposed Solution

The Enterprise AI Business Intelligence Copilot will allow NovaMart employees to ask questions using natural language.

Example:

User:

"Show me total sales in Kerala."

The AI will:

1. Understand the question.
2. Identify the required data.
3. Generate an SQL query.
4. Validate the SQL query.
5. Execute the query.
6. Retrieve the result.
7. Display the result.
8. Generate a chart when appropriate.
9. Explain the result in simple business language.

## 6. Example Business Question

User:

"Which state generated the highest sales?"

AI process:

Natural Language Question
        ↓
AI / LLM
        ↓
SQL Generation
        ↓
SQL Validation
        ↓
PostgreSQL Database
        ↓
Query Result
        ↓
Chart
        ↓
AI Business Explanation
