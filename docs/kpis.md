# Key Performance Indicators

## Sales KPIs

### Total Revenue

Total amount of revenue generated.

### Total Sales

Total sales value generated by the company.

### Total Orders

Total number of orders.

---

## Profit KPIs

### Total Profit

Total profit generated by the company.

### Profit Margin

Percentage of revenue retained as profit.

Formula:

Profit Margin = (Profit / Revenue) × 100

---

## Customer KPIs

### Total Customers

Number of customers in the database.

### Average Customer Revenue

Average revenue generated per customer.

---

## Product KPIs

### Total Products

Number of products available.

### Top Product

Product with the highest sales.

---

## Regional KPIs

### Top State

State with the highest sales.

### Top Region

Region with the highest sales.

---

## Target KPIs

### Target Achievement

Percentage of sales target achieved.

Formula:

Target Achievement = (Actual Sales / Sales Target) × 100
