# Reporting Architecture

## Reporting Flow

Dashboard / Analysis
↓
Selected KPIs
↓
Charts
↓
Executive Summary
↓
PDF Generator
↓
Business Report

---

## Reporting Responsibilities

The reporting engine will:

- Collect selected KPIs
- Collect analysis results
- Collect charts
- Generate executive summaries
- Generate business reports
- Generate PDF reports

---

## Reporting Inputs

The reporting engine will receive:

- KPI data
- Sales analysis
- Customer analysis
- Product analysis
- Regional analysis
- Forecast results
- Charts
- AI-generated business insights

---

## Reporting Output

The reporting engine will generate:

- Business reports
- Executive summaries
- Tables
- Charts
- PDF reports
