# System Features

## 1. AI Business Intelligence Chat

Users can ask business questions using natural language.

Example:

"Show total sales for Kerala."

---

## 2. Natural Language to SQL

The AI converts natural language questions into SQL queries.

---

## 3. SQL Query Execution

The generated SQL query is executed against the business database.

---

## 4. SQL Validation

The system validates generated SQL before execution.

The MVP will allow safe read-only queries.

---

## 5. KPI Dashboard

The dashboard will display:

- Total Revenue
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

---

## 6. Sales Analytics

The system will analyze:

- Daily sales
- Monthly sales
- Quarterly sales
- Yearly sales
- Sales by state
- Sales by region
- Sales by product

---

## 7. Customer Analytics

The system will analyze:

- Total customers
- Top customers
- Customer revenue
- Customer purchase behavior
- Customer trends

---

## 8. Product Analytics

The system will analyze:

- Best-selling products
- Most profitable products
- Product categories
- Product revenue
- Product performance

---

## 9. Regional Analytics

The system will analyze:

- State performance
- Regional performance
- Sales by state
- Profit by state
- Region comparison

---

## 10. AI Business Summary

The AI will summarize business results in simple language.

---

## 11. Sales Forecasting

The system will estimate future sales using historical sales data.

---

## 12. Financial Report Analysis

Users will be able to upload financial reports for analysis.

---

## 13. PDF Report Generation

The system will generate reports containing:

- KPIs
- Tables
- Charts
- AI summaries

---

## 14. Chart Explanation

The AI will explain important patterns in charts.

---

## 15. Voice Input

Users will be able to ask business questions using voice.

---

## 16. Power BI Integration

Power BI will be used for advanced dashboards and visualization.
