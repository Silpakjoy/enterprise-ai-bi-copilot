## Key Business Metrics

### Revenue

Measures total sales generated by the business.

### Profit

Measures the difference between revenue and cost.

### Profit Margin

Measures profit as a percentage of revenue.

### Orders

Measures the number of orders placed.

### Customers

Measures customer activity and customer count.

### Growth

Measures changes in business performance over time.
